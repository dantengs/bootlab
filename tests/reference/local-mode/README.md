`dynamical_sdp-resumed.out`: the iteration table of a dynamical_sdp call resumed from the checkpoint a
`bin/bootlab stop` left (Ising Lambda 11, step 1, 96 ranks, local mode on the development cluster; the lines of
its output other than the table and its end are left out). Its numbers start at 1 and end at 110, the call's
iterations; the uninterrupted call made 144, the stopped one 34 (tests/test_local_mode.py Iterations).
